PalMeet 0.2.6

  • Google Meet: speakers are named from the speaking highlight on their tile (the moving voice bars), even with captions off. Update the PalMeet extension in Chrome (it reloads itself) to get this.
  • Meet's "Are you talking? Your mic is off." is no longer read as a person called "Are you". Voices learned under such names are removed.
  • Your own tile is ignored when naming remote speakers, and captions win over the highlight.
  • Review → Regenerate → "Transcribe again from the audio" redoes a meeting's transcript and summary.
