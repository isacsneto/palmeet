PalMeet 0.2.2

  • A meeting is recorded only once. Pressing the record hotkey again while a recording starts no longer starts extra recordings, and PalMeet never records a whole Chrome window by itself when the meeting tab can be recorded through the extension.
  • Your voice in another call stays out: if you're unmuted in a Teams or Zoom call that PalMeet isn't recording, a meeting that can't report its mute state doesn't get your mic. Give PalMeet Accessibility access so it can read Teams' mute button.
  • Clear banner while Chrome waits for the one click on the PalMeet icon (or ⌥⇧R), and Chrome brings the meeting tab forward.
  • Updates now wait until your recordings finish before restarting PalMeet.
